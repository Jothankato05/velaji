Where Nigeria fails to vaccinate, it also fails to register

Two national failures, one child. They share a cause.

Lowest birth registration

They are never written down

  • Sokoto 22.5%  ·  Jigawa 23.6%
  • Against Lagos 93.7% and FCT 87.3%
  • Nigeria holds 11% of West Africa's unregistered children
Lowest immunisation

They are never vaccinated

  • Sokoto 11% Penta3  ·  Zamfara 25%
  • Against Lagos 85% and FCT 80%
  • 51% of Sokoto's children have had no vaccine at all

Correlation of 0.70 across all 37 states — eleven fall below the national average on both. A child born at home without a skilled attendant is missed by the register and missed by the cold chain on the same day: zero-dose runs at 35.4% for home deliveries against 16.7% in facilities.

Source: MICS / NICS 2021, all 37 states, one survey. Pearson r = 0.70; twelve states break the pattern, Katsina and Bayelsa most visibly. Delivery-place figures from a six-state determinants study (n = 1,958). Team Primers  ·  03

The fund built for these children cannot reach them

A consequence nobody planned, and the reason an identifier is the intervention.

The entitlement exists

₦282.7bn, already committed

  • BHCPF is 1% of the Consolidated Revenue Fund — ₦125.7bn in 2024, ₦282.7bn in 2025
  • Its Vulnerable Group Fund covers under-fives and pregnant women
  • NHIA Act 2022 made health insurance mandatory
The door is shut

Enrolment runs on the NIN

  • You enrol through the social register and the NIN
  • No birth registration means no NIN
  • So the children with the strongest claim are the ones who cannot make it

Nigeria is not short of money for this. What is missing is the identifier that connects a child to it.

Sources: National Health Insurance Authority; Basic Health Care Provision Fund, NPHCDA gateway; BudgIT budget analysis, 2025. Team Primers  ·  04

One number. One card. It works like a bank card

The card carries the account, works at any branch, and the machine knows what to do.

Register child→ Card prints→ Present at any facility→ System says what's next→ Dose recorded→ NHIA coverage activates

USSD access

A short code on a feature phone. No smartphone, no app, no data — where coverage is worst, that is the phone people have.

Offline-first

Mobile teams register and record doses with no connectivity, syncing when they return to range.

Opens before birth

Registered at antenatal care, where under four visits roughly doubles zero-dose risk.

The parent remembers nothing — and the number is what carries her into civil registration, and into the coverage she was owed.

Antenatal figures: six-state determinants study (n = 1,958); WHO 2016 antenatal care model. Around 37% of Nigerian women attend no antenatal care at all, so this is an additional route in, never the only one. Team Primers  ·  05

What adoption costs: scan one QR code

That is the whole change to a health worker's day. Nothing already deployed has to move.

A layer, not a replacement

We build to EMID, not around it

  • Nigeria already runs EMID for immunisation data, Gavi-funded
  • NPHCDA publishes an Immunization FHIR guide — we export to it
  • EMID keeps the record; we add what it does not hold
Less work, not more

The scan replaces a slower step

  • The worker already needs to know which vaccine is due
  • Today they derive it from a paper card, or a guess
  • No new hardware — the card is printed paper

Velaji adds the two things a reporting system does not hold: something in the mother's hand, and what each child is due next.

Sources: Electronic Management of Immunization Data evaluation, Global Health: Science and Practice, 2024; NPHCDA Immunization FHIR Implementation Guide. Team Primers  ·  06

Two engines. Neither works alone

One spreads without us. The other is already funded by law.

Growth

It spreads on its own

  • The card is free to the family, so every facility that scans one adds to the national record without being told to
  • Registration is not facility-only — home births, community health workers, mobile teams and outreach all enter the same registry
Sustainability

Funded by statute, not philanthropy

  • NHIA Act 2022 and 1% of the Consolidated Revenue Fund already target zero-dose reduction in the first year of life
  • Complete the schedule and coverage activates — we build the rail for money that already exists

Without the reward, the card is just admin. Without the record, the reward cannot be verified — so it cannot be paid.

Nigeria transitions off Gavi support by 2028, having committed to increase vaccine co-financing 10% a year. Every naira has to buy more coverage. Team Primers  ·  07
