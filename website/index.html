The problem

The children who miss vaccines are often the children no system can see.

2.3Mzero-dose children in Nigeria, who have received no routine vaccine. The second highest number in the world.UNICEF
71% → 57%coverage falls from the first vaccine (DTP1) to the first measles dose (MCV1). Children start the schedule and then drop out.WHO / UNICEF 2024
57%of under-five births are registered. A child without a birth record has no national ID, and can't be enrolled in the health fund meant for her.UNICEF / MICS 2021

The immunisation record, civil registration and health insurance each fail the same child without sharing an identifier. Velaji is built for that gap.

The platform

One record that follows the child, and work queues that follow the gaps.

A health ID for every child

Each child gets a Child Health Identification Number (CHIN) with a check character, so a mistyped or misheard ID is caught instead of pointing at the wrong child. It is not a national ID, and works before a child has one.

A card that can't be forged

A printable card with a signed QR code. Scanning it shows status and schedule without a staff login, and a tampered code is rejected.

Five-colour status at a glance

Velaji builds the full routine schedule from the date of birth and shows where every child stands.

  • On track
  • Due soon
  • Overdue
  • Needs review
  • Complete

Reminders that don't spam

One message per child, leading with the most overdue vaccine, only in daytime, with a cap on attempts. Children who stay unreachable are escalated to people, not texted forever.

A tracing queue for health workers

Every escalated child becomes a workable item with the caregiver's phone, facility, vaccine and how overdue it is. Recording the missing dose closes it automatically.

Works offline

A health worker's device keeps working without a network and syncs when the connection returns, designed for the connectivity facilities actually have.

Follows the child between facilities

When a family moves or turns up at another clinic, the record moves with them, and Velaji can find the nearest known facility to where they are.

Least privilege, full audit

A verifier sees status only. A health worker sees what's needed to continue care. Every lookup is written to an audit trail that admins can review.

A command view without personal data

Coverage, dropout, zero-dose counts, upcoming vaccine demand and outlier facilities, drillable from national to state, LGA, ward and facility. Counts and rates only, never individual children.

Any phone

Caregivers without a smartphone are first-class users.

Low phone ownership and literacy mean text alone isn't enough. Velaji serves caregivers over USSD on any basic phone: dial in, see the child's status, the next vaccine and where to go. The screens shown use the exact wording Velaji sends, with an example child.

Who it's for

Built to pilot with the people who run immunisation.

State primary health care boards

Priority triage of the worst-performing LGAs and facilities, zero-dose and dropout tracking, and vaccine demand forecasts for stock planning.

LGAs and health facilities

Registration at the point of care, a daily tracing queue, and status checks by card or ID in seconds.

Development partners and NGOs

A recovery workflow for zero-dose and defaulting children, with the real barrier captured on each traced case: hesitancy, distance, insecurity and more.

Health insurance schemes

A completion record that can support incentives like a "Healthy Start" reward, positioned as health coverage rather than payment for vaccination.

Where Velaji stands

A working platform, honest about its seams.

Velaji is a working prototype, tested end to end, and ready for pilots. It is not commissioned by, affiliated with or endorsed by any agency. Connections to national systems, such as civil registration, national identity and health insurance settlement, need authority and agreements that software alone can't provide. Velaji builds each of these as a visible, clearly marked connection point to be switched on with the right partners, never a faked one. It is designed to work alongside Nigeria's existing immunisation data systems, not replace them.
