Privacy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy covers this website, getvelaji.primersgroup.ng, run by Primers Group, Abuja, Nigeria. The Velaji platform itself handles children's health records under its own controls: least-privilege access, an audit trail of every lookup, and a national view that shows counts and rates only, never individual children.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, advertising or analytics. Our hosting provider, Vercel, records basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Staff sign-in with Primers ID

Facility staff can connect a Primers ID (id.primersgroup.ng) to their Velaji staff account and then sign in with it. Primers ID confirms the person's name and verified email to Velaji and gives an account number used only to link the two accounts. Velaji never receives the Primers ID password, and Primers ID receives nothing from Velaji's health records.

Contacting us

If you email us to request a pilot, we use your name, email and message only to reply and to arrange the pilot.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Email primerscorperation@gmail.com.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.
